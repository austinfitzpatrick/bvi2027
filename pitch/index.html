Getting There

  • Via Miami: LAX → MIA on American, then AA's E175 nonstop to Beef Island/Tortola (EIS). One ticket, one connection, lands 10 minutes from the charter base.
  • Via San Juan (SJU): one-stop to San Juan, then a 40-minute InterCaribbean or Cape Air puddle-jumper to Beef Island. Lands right at the charter base, but two separate bookings.
  • Via St. Thomas (STT): one-stop on American, Delta, or United (usually through MIA or JFK), then a ferry to the BVI (~$150/person round trip) or a private water taxi (~$500/person round trip). Cheapest all-in. Budget a 5-hour buffer between ferry pickup and your return flight.

Note: if we pick Minted One, the charter runs out of St. Thomas instead — that makes the STT route the obvious one.
